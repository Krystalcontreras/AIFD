Baruch College SSEP Mission Patch Generator

Core Mission Concept & Narrative

This tool generates modular, customizable 3.5″ × 3.5″ mission patches representing Baruch College's participation in scientific research aboard the International Space Station (ISS) for the SSEP competition.

Central Narrative Symbol: The Baruch College Bearcat mascot being tractor-beamed up into an alien spacecraft, symbolizing student curiosity, research extraction, and being lifted beyond Earth into weightlessness/microgravity.

Celestial Elements:

Top-Left Anchor: Moon displaying surface craters.

Background Dynamics: Passing "Astros" / shooting meteor trails cutting across the sky.

Atmospheric/Cosmic Base: Volumetric, stylized clouds representing deep space nebulas.

Core Question Addressed: "What message would Baruch send into space?" — A bold, playful, yet scientifically curious representation of Baruch’s vibrant NYC community reaching beyond the atmosphere.

Technical Specifications & Geometry Rules

Output Canvas

Dimensions: 1:1 Aspect Ratio square frame, rendered at 3.5″ × 3.5″ (exportable at minimum 1000 × 1000 px PNG).

Border Structure: Classic embroidered mission patch border (inner ring + outer edge line with adjustable border stroke width).

Layout Composition Modes

Classic Centered Abduction (Default):

Top Left: Moon element.

Center Top: UFO / Alien Spaceship.

Center Mid/Low: Tractor beam extending downward, surrounding the levitating Bearcat.

Background Layer: Nebula cloud clusters with passing astros/shooting stars angled across the upper and lower quadrants.

Diagonal Orbit Thrust:

UFO positioned upper-right, beaming down at a 45-degree angle toward the Bearcat in the bottom-left corner. Moon placed in top-left with comets sweeping diagonally.

Framed Inverted Beam:

Central focus on the Bearcat inside a full-height beam, flanked by twin nebula clouds and lunar crescents.

Typography & Text Integration

Text Zones

Patch Header / Arch Text: Top curved or straight banner text reading BARUCH COLLEGE or BARUCH SSEP.

Mission Sub-Header: Bottom border text reading SPRING 2027 or MICROGRAVITY RESEARCH.

Callout Tag / Patch Emblem: Optional badge tag reading NYC TO ISS or MISSION #01.

Font Options

Display / Patch Headers: Bold, impact space typography (e.g., Archivo Black, Space Grotesk, Fredoka One, or Changa One).

Monospace / Technical Labels: Clean data text (e.g., Cutive Mono).

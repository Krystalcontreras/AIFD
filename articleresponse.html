READING RESPONSE — JARON LANIER

Oy, A.I. Reading Response

After reading Jaron Lanier’s “Oy, A.I.,” I thought his perspective on artificial intelligence was interesting, especially the way he discusses how AI depends on human creativity. I agree with his point that AI should not be seen as something that can completely replace people. AI is able to generate images, writing, and ideas, but all of that is based on information and creativity that originally came from humans. As someone studying graphic design, I think this is important because designing something is more than just creating a finished product. There is a thought process, personal experience, and individual perspective behind the work that makes it meaningful. AI can be useful as a tool, but I don't think it should take away the value of the people who create the original ideas.

I also think Lanier brings up an important point about giving credit to the people whose work helps AI develop. Artists, writers, and other creators put time and effort into their work, so their contributions should not be overlooked just because technology can now produce something similar. Personally, I don't think AI is necessarily a bad thing, and I can see how it can make certain tasks easier. However, I think there needs to be a balance between using AI and maintaining human creativity. Technology should help us develop our ideas, not replace the originality and individuality that people bring to their work.

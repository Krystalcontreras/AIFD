Campaign Launch: The Golden Cider Hour

Campaign Overview & Brief

Product Focus: Pout Preserve Peptide Lip Treatment in Spiced Apple Cider

NYC Launch Strategy: The Golden Cider Hour positions Olehenriksen’s cult-favorite Pout Preserve Peptide Lip Treatment as an indispensable winter hydration luxury across New York City. The campaign bridges Scandinavian clinical skincare performance with the sensory warmth of fresh Hudson Valley pressed apples, brown sugar drip, and grated clove.

Creative Core: Designed to contrast the crisp, chilly air of NYC winter with an ultra-dewy, high-shine amber glow. Key NYC activation touchpoints include a Scandinavian pop-up cider press kiosk in Bryant Park, high-impact wildposting runs in Soho and Bushwick, and weather-triggered digital LinkNYC displays that intensify warmth and luminescence as ambient city temperatures plunge below freezing.
